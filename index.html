MotorLoop 4.0 · How to Play
Before you start
Goal — Build a complete Digital Product Passport for the BM-220 motor and reach a Smart Circular Economy.
The loop — In each phase, sell a batch of motors to earn POPS, then invest POPS and Tech Points in technologies that fill your DPP fields.
Motors can be sold — Once in Phase 0 and Phase 3 and twice in Phase 1 and Phase 2.
Phase 0 — Learn all 8 DPP infrastructure modules, then pass the quiz (6 of 8) to unlock the factory.
Phase 1 — Install the first 4 foundational technologies (Communication, AAS, Data Space, QR/UID) to start building your DPP.
Phase 2 — Add advanced technologies (Cloud, IoT, Blockchain, Digital Twin) to complete your Digital Product Passport.
Phase 3 — Activate circular economy strategies to push your SCE score to 100% and win.
Technologies — Unlock in a chain, starting with Communication Tech. Click a card to read what it does, then Install it. Locked cards show what they still need.
Win — Fill all 6 DPP fields and install the smart technologies to push your SCE score toward 100%.
